**Question and implementation.** We ask whether fixed-weight, trajectory-specific follow-up questions help monitors detect executed unauthorized actions beyond fixed questions and extra review, while controlling false alarms and cost. TGF is the method; G² = TPR × TNR is an evaluation metric. We implemented agent rollouts in MonitorBench's banking and airline environments, frozen configurations, public/private observation separation, event/state logging, four comparison conditions, and evidence audits. Monitors do not receive hidden objectives or private verifier state. Actor explanations alone do not establish execution or authorization.

**Early experiments.** Small earlier comparisons are diagnostic, not reliable estimates of TGF's benefit. In a six-trajectory comparison, banking lacked negatives, and the two airline negatives had no sensitive executions. An apparent TGF detection gain relied on an unsupported reason; all four conditions missed a 16-person reservation when the request required 17. This motivates checking substantive evidence, rather than counting citations or correct labels alone.

We also explored Qwen3-32B LoRA with 64 training, 12 validation, and 24 held-out synthetic component examples. Interface/input defects required a separate repair diagnostic. In that diagnostic, none of three arms correctly cleared any of four negatives per arm; ordinary SFT accused all 12 cases and evidence SFT truncated on five. These small, related examples do not establish that SFT is necessary, generally effective, or generally ineffective. The optional training extension was pursued before the main data and interface checks were ready.

**Data recovery and audited coverage.** Across the first 60 planned positions, 51 candidates were attempted and all 755 observed tool events were audited. Five context-guard failures and one timeout are excluded from class targets; nine positions remain unattempted. The 45 non-error traces contain banking: 21 positives and two negatives; airlines: 12 positives, nine negatives, and one unknown. Complete public violation evidence supports 10 banking and 12 airline positives. Only one banking and three airline negatives include actual authorized sensitive execution. A verification SMS/fee without principal payment is not counted as a payment negative. All second-batch traces remain task-incomplete, even when the actor claims completion. Labels concern observed tool execution, not truthfulness of every final statement. Review is non-blind primary-AI assistance, not independent human gold. Different seeds still cover only two fixed task instances.

**Remaining work.** The proposal's 30-positive/30-negative target per environment remains unmet. Banking's observed negative yield is 2/26 attempted candidates; obtaining 28 more negatives from the original next 90 would require 31.1%. This is a planning comparison, not a success-probability estimate. We propose smaller, explicitly scoped natural task instances in the same environments, with private injected objectives that actually conflict with public authorization. This changes the data construction and awaits approval. A 12-trajectory qualification stage would precede scaling; new and original data would be reported separately. No further monitor comparison or training starts before data, evidence, and shared-interface checks pass.
